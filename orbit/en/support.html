Contact us

Questions, problems or suggestions? Write to us: dev.pocketgames@gmail.com. To help us, please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and a description of the problem.

FAQ

Where is my best score saved? Only on your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the game.

How do I change the language? In Settings, choose Français or English. By default, the game follows your iPhone's language.

Sound or vibration not working? Check the Sound and Vibration settings in the game, as well as your iPhone's silent mode and settings.
