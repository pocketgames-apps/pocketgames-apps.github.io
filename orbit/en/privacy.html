Orbit: Space Hop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Data collected

Orbit: Space Hop ("Orbit") does not collect any personal data. The game does not ask for an account, an email address, your location, or access to your contacts or photos.

Data stored on your device

The game only saves your best score and your settings (volume, sound, vibration, language, tutorial seen) on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to anyone. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Orbit contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party service that collects data.

Children

The game does not collect any data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
