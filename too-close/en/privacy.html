Too Close: Risk It! - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Data collected

Too Close: Risk It! ("Too Close") does not collect any personal data. The game does not ask for an account, an email address, your location, or access to your contacts or photos.

Data stored on your device

The game only saves your best score, your settings (volume, vibration, language) and your progress in the game (intro already seen, character appearance) on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to anyone. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Too Close contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party service that collects data. The game works offline.

Children

The game does not collect any data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
